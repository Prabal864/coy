# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Coy is for adults who are curious about someone they already like, especially socially reserved people and people who share a college, workplace, friend group, or community. They use Discover on a phone to explore possible connections without the pressure of approaching someone directly.

## Product Purpose

Coy helps a user express interest privately and learn whether that interest is mutual. A successful session preserves the user's privacy, makes the matching rules clear, and ends with either a mutual connection or confidence that an unreciprocated like was not exposed.

## Positioning

Find out if your crush likes you back without having to make the first move.

## Brand Personality

Warm, private, optimistic. The experience should feel calm and human rather than performative, competitive, or noisy.

## Anti-references

This should not feel like a public dating catalogue, a gamified swipe loop, or a social feed that reveals one-sided interest. Avoid pressure, rejection-forward language, and attention-grabbing mechanics that make privacy feel secondary.

## Design Principles

- Privacy is the product promise: only mutual interest is revealed.
- Curiosity should feel safe, calm, and low-pressure.
- Every action should explain what happens next.
- A match is a meaningful moment, not a reward loop.
- The interface should stay warm and human while remaining direct and easy to use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear labels, strong text contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, and a reduced-motion alternative. Do not rely on color alone to communicate like/pass state. The product is intended for adults 18+ and should avoid implying that a non-match is a personal rejection.
